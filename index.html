💪 Beginner Gym Workout Plan

Simple weekly plan for muscle gain

Monday – Chest

• Bench Press – 4 × 8
• Incline Dumbbell Press – 3 × 10
• Cable Fly – 3 × 12

Tuesday – Back

• Lat Pulldown – 4 × 10
• Seated Row – 3 × 10
• Dumbbell Row – 3 × 10

Wednesday – Legs

• Squats – 4 × 8
• Leg Press – 3 × 10
• Leg Curl – 3 × 12

Thursday – Shoulders

• Dumbbell Shoulder Press – 4 × 10
• Lateral Raise – 3 × 12
• Shrugs – 3 × 12

Friday – Arms

• Barbell Curl – 3 × 10
• Hammer Curl – 3 × 12
• Tricep Pushdown – 3 × 12

Diet Tip

Eat eggs, milk, banana, peanut butter, chicken, paneer and dal daily. Drink plenty of water and sleep 7–8 hours.
